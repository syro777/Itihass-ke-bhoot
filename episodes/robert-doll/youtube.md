📜 Facts: GTA 6 Leonida naam ke kaalpanik state mein set hai, jo Florida par aadharit hai, aur uska Leonida Keys ilaaka asli Florida Keys jaisa hai. Florida Keys ke Key West mein Robert the Doll hai: lagbhag 1904 ki ek sailor-suit wali gudiya, jo artist Robert Eugene 'Gene' Otto ki thi. Lokkatha (kehte hain): ghar mein kuch toot-ta to Gene kehta 'Robert ne kiya', aur padosi gudiya ko khidki par jagah badalte dekhne ki baat karte the. 1994 se Robert Key West ke Fort East Martello Museum mein hai. Museum kehta hai ki photo lene se pehle Robert se ijaazat maango, aur kai log badkismati ke baad use maafi ki chitthiyan bhejte hain. Kisi bhoot ka koi saboot nahi hai; ye ek lokkatha hai.
