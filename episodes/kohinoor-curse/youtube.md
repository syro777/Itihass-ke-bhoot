📜 Facts: Koh-i-Noor ka matlab hai 'roshni ka pahaad'. Maana jaata hai ki ye Golconda ilaake ki khadaanon se aaya. Ye Mughalon ke Mayur Singhasan (Peacock Throne) mein jada tha; 1739 mein Nadir Shah ise le gaya aur 1747 mein uski hatya hui. Baad mein ye Maharaja Ranjit Singh ke paas pahuncha, aur 1849 mein British ne ise 10 saal ke Maharaja Duleep Singh se le liya. Lokkatha kehti hai ki ise sirf Bhagwan ya ek aurat hi surakshit pehen sakti hai; Britain mein ise sirf raniyon ne pehna. Aaj ye Queen Mother ke taaj mein jada hai aur Tower of London mein rakha hai. 'Shraap' ek lokkatha hai, saabit tathya nahi.
