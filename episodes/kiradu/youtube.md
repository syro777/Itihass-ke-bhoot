📜 Facts: Kiradu ke mandir Barmer zile (Rajasthan) mein hain aur 11vi–12vi sadi ke maane jaate hain (lagbhag 900 saal purane). Aaj sirf 5 mandir khade hain, aur inki nakkashi ki tulna Khajuraho se hoti hai, isliye inhe 'Rajasthan ka Khajuraho' kehte hain. Lokkatha: ek sadhu ke beemar shishya ki kisi ne madad nahi ki, sirf ek kumhaarin ne seva ki. Naraaz sadhu ne gaon ko patthar banne ka shraap diya aur kumhaarin se kaha ki peechhe mat dekhna; usne dekha aur wo bhi patthar ban gayi. Is shraap ka koi saboot nahi hai, par aaj bhi log shaam ke baad yahan nahi rukte.
