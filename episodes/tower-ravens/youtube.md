📜 Facts: Tower of London mein kauve (ravens) rakhe jaate hain. Lokkatha (legend) ke mutabik agar kauvon ne Tower chhoda, to Tower aur raajgaddi dono gir jaayenge. Isliye kam se kam 6 kauve hamesha rakhe jaate hain (Historic Royal Palaces ke mutabik abhi 8 hain), aur ek Ravenmaster unki dekhbhaal karta hai; unke pankh kabhi-kabhi halke se trim kiye jaate hain. Kahani kehti hai ki King Charles II ne kauvon ko rakhne ka hukm diya, par ye bhi legend hi hai. Tower ke itihaaskaar Geoffrey Parnell ke mutabik ye legend shayad Victorian daur mein bana.
