📜 Facts: Isla de las Muñecas (Island of the Dolls) Mexico City ke Xochimilco nehron mein hai. Iske dekhbhaal karne wale Don Julián Santana Barrera yahan akele rehte the aur lagbhag 50 saal tak saikdon gudiyan pedon par latkaate rahe. Kehte hain unhe paani mein doobi ek bachchi mili thi, aur phir ek tairti gudiya, jise unhone uski aatma ke liye latka diya. 2001 mein unki maut hui; bataya jaata hai ki wo usi jagah nehar mein doobe jahan unhone bachchi dekhne ki baat kahi thi.
