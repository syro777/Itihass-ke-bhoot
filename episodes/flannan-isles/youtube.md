📜 Facts: Flannan Isles Lighthouse Scotland ke Outer Hebrides mein Eilean Mòr taapu par hai (1899 mein shuru hua). December 1900 mein yahan teen rakhwale the: James Ducat, Thomas Marshall aur Donald MacArthur. Slate par aakhri entry 15 December ki thi, aur usi raat paas se guzarte steamer Archtor ne dekha ki lighthouse ki batti bujhi hui hai. Kharaab mausam ki vajah se relief jahaaz Hesperus 26 December ko pahuncha, aur taapu par koi nahi mila. Andar sirf ek rakhwale ka barsaati coat (oilskins) bacha tha. Superintendent Robert Muirhead ne paschimi landing par toofaan ka bhaari nuksaan dekha aur andaaza lagaya ki teeno ko ek vishaal lehar baha le gayi. Teeno ke shareer kabhi nahi mile. 'Adhoora khaana' aur 'ulti kursi' wali baatein 1912 ki Wilfrid Gibson ki kavita 'Flannan Isle' se aayi, asli report se nahi.
