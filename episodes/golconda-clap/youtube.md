📜 Facts: Golconda Fort Hyderabad (Telangana) mein hai. Kehte hain Fateh Darwaza ke gumbad ke neeche bajaayi taali upar Bala Hisar pavilion tak sunaai deti hai, jo kareeb 1 km door hai, aur ise khatre ka alarm maana jaata tha. Darwaze par haathiyon ko rokne ke liye nukeele lohe ke keele lage hain. Qutb Shahi daur mein Golconda heeron ke vyaapaar ke liye mashhoor tha. 1687 mein Aurangzeb ne mahinon ki gherabandi ke baad qila jeeta.
