📜 Facts: Chand Baori Abhaneri gaon (Dausa zila, Rajasthan) mein hai. Isme lagbhag 3,500 seedhiyan aur 13 manzil hain, aur ye kareeb 100 feet gehri hai. Maana jaata hai ki ise 8vi–9vi sadi mein Nikumbh vansh ke Raja Chanda ne banwaya, par nirmaan ka koi shilalekh nahi mila. Ise baarish ka paani jama karne ke liye banaya gaya tha, aur neeche ki hawa upar se thandi rehti hai. 'Bhooton ne ek raat mein banaya' aur 'jis seedhi se utre, usse wapas nahi chadh paate' — ye dono lokkatha (legend) hain.
