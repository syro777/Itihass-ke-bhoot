📜 Facts: 8 March 2014 ko Malaysia Airlines ki flight MH370 (Boeing 777) Kuala Lumpur se Beijing ja rahi thi, jisme 239 log the. Udaan ke kareeb 40 minute baad aakhiri radio sandesh aaya: 'Good night, Malaysian three seven zero'. Phir transponder band ho gaya. Military radar ne dekha ki plane wapas mudkar Malaysia ke upar se Andaman Sagar ki taraf gaya. Inmarsat satellite ke automatic 'handshake' signals se pata chala ki plane take-off ke kareeb 7 ghante baad tak udta raha aur dakshini Hind Mahasagar mein khatam hua. July 2015 mein Réunion dweep par uske pankh ka ek hissa (flaperon) mila. 2014–2017 ki khoj mein 1,20,000 sq km samundari tal chhaana gaya; 2018 aur 2025–26 mein Ocean Infinity ne bhi khoja, par vimaan nahi mila. June 2026 mein Malaysia ne 'no find, no fee' deal ek saal badhayi: vimaan mila to 70 million dollar, nahi to kuch nahi.
