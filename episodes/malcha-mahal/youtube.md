📜 Facts: Malcha Mahal (Wilayat Mahal) Delhi ke Ridge jungle (Chanakyapuri) mein Tughlaq daur ka ek shikargaah hai. Begum Wilayat Mahal ne khud ko Awadh ke shahi gharane ki waaris bataya aur 1970 ke dashak se kareeb 10 saal New Delhi railway station ke waiting room mein harjaane ke liye dharna diya. 1985 mein sarkaar ne unhe Malcha Mahal de diya. Parivaar wahan bina bijli-paani ke, duniya se kat kar raha. Begum ki 1993 mein maut hui; beti Sakina kuch saal pehle chal basi, aur beta Ali Raza ('Cyrus') 2 September 2017 ko mahal mein akela mrit mila. 2019 mein New York Times ki patrakar Ellen Barry ki jaanch ('The Jungle Prince of Delhi') mein saamne aaya ki is parivaar ka Awadh ke shahi gharane se koi rishta nahi tha. Mahal mein 'ajeeb aawaazon' ki baatein sirf sthaniya kahaniyan hain.
