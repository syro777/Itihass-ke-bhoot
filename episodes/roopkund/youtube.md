## Description
Himalaya mein 16,000 feet se bhi upar ek jheel… jisme saikdon insaani kankaal pade hain! 😱
1942 mein ek forest ranger ne inhe dekha. Pehle maana gaya ki sab ek oley ke toofan mein mare — par 2019 ki DNA jaanch ne sabko chaunka diya!

📜 Facts: Roopkund jheel Uttarakhand ke Chamoli zile mein lagbhag 5,029 m ki oonchai par hai. 2019 ki study (Nature Communications) mein 38 kankaalon ka DNA dekha gaya: kuch log Bharat ke the jo lagbhag 1200 saal pehle mare, aur 14 log Greece ke aaspaas (Eastern Mediterranean) ke the jo lagbhag 200 saal pehle mare.
