## Description
1985 mein Faizabad mein ek sadhu ki maut hui… aur unke kamre se Netaji ke parivaar ki tasveerein mili! 😱
Sarkaari kahani kehti hai Netaji ki maut 1945 ke plane crash mein hui. Phir 2005 mein Mukherjee Aayog ne kaha — wo us crash mein nahi mare! To kya Gumnaami Baba hi Netaji the?

Aapko kya lagta hai — 1945 ke baad Netaji ka kya hua? 👇 Comment mein batao!

📜 Facts: Shah Nawaz Committee aur Khosla Commission ne 1945 ke Taihoku plane crash mein maut maani. Mukherjee Aayog (2005) ne kaha Netaji us crash mein nahi mare; sarkaar ne 2006 mein ye report nahi maani. Vishnu Sahai Aayog ki report (2019 mein saamne aayi) ke mutabik Gumnaami Baba Netaji nahi, unke anuyayi the — haalanki unki aawaaz Netaji jaisi thi. Daanton ke DNA test ka nateeja bhi Netaji ke parivaar se mel nahi khaya.
