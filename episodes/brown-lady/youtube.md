📜 Facts: Raynham Hall England ke Norfolk mein ek purani shahi haveli hai. Lokkatha (legend) ke mutabik yahan ki 'Brown Lady' Lady Dorothy Walpole (Townshend) ki aatma hai, jinki 1726 mein maut hui; kahani kehti hai ki unke pati ne unhe kamre mein band kar diya tha. Log bhoore kapdon wali ek aurat ko seedhiyon par dekhne ki baat karte hain, aur kehte hain ek captain ne us par goli bhi chalayi thi. 19 September 1936 ko Country Life magazine ke photographer Captain Hubert Provand aur Indra Shira seedhiyon ki photo le rahe the, jab Shira ne kaha ki seedhi par koi dhundhli aakriti utar rahi hai. Ye photo December 1936 mein Country Life aur 1937 mein Life magazine mein chhapi aur duniya bhar mein mashhoor hui. Jaankar (jaise Joe Nickell) kehte hain ki ye shayad double exposure tha; pakka saboot kisi bhi taraf nahi hai. (Video ki tasveerein 3D animation hain, asli photo nahi.)
