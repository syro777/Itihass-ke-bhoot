📜 Facts aur lokkathayein:
• Dumas Beach (Surat): kaali ret ki wajah bhaari khanij (jaise iron) bataye jaate hain. Shmashaan wali baat aur logon ke gaayab hone ki kahaniyan lokkatha hain, inka pakka record nahi milta.
• Dow Hill (Kurseong): Dow Hill School ki shuruaat 1879 mein hui. Bina sir wale ladke ki kahani lokkatha hai; school prashaasan ke mutabik campus par aisi koi maut nahi hui.
• Shaniwar Wada (Pune): neev 1730 mein Peshwa Bajirao I ne rakhi. 30 August 1773 ko 18 saal ke Peshwa Narayanrao ki hatya hui. 1828 ki aag lagbhag 7 din jali. 'Dhara-maara' aur 'Kaka mala vachva' ki cheekh lokkatha hai.
• Kuldhara (Jaisalmer): Paliwal Brahmin 13vi sadi ke aaspaas yahan base; gaon lagbhag 1825 mein khaali hua. Salim Singh aur 84 gaon ki ek raat wali kahani lokkatha hai; itihaaskaar paani ki kami aur bhaari tax ko wajah maante hain.
• Bhangarh (Alwar): 16vi sadi mein Raja Bhagwant Das ne Madho Singh ke liye banwaaya. Guru Balu Nath aur tantrik Singhia ki kahaniyan lokkatha hain. Puratattva vibhaag ka board suryoday se pehle aur suryast ke baad pravesh mana karta hai.
