📜 Facts: Kailash Mandir (Ellora Gufa No. 16) Maharashtra ke Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar (Aurangabad) zile mein hai aur UNESCO World Heritage Ellora Caves ka hissa hai. Ise ek hi basalt chattaan ko upar se neeche ki or kaatkar banaya gaya; isme koi eent ya jod nahi hai, aur ise duniya ki sabse badi monolithic (ek patthar se bani) rock-cut sanrachnaon mein gina jaata hai. Koi daan-shilalekh nahi mila, par aam taur par ise 8vi sadi ke Rashtrakut raja Krishna I ke samay ka maana jaata hai. Anumaan hai ki lagbhag 2 laakh ton patthar hataya gaya (alag-alag sources mein anumaan alag hain), aur ye patthar kahan gaya, ye sawaal aaj bhi poocha jaata hai. Mandir Bhagwan Shiv ko samarpit hai aur Kailash parvat jaisa banaya gaya. Aliens wali baatein sirf afwaah hain; itihaaskaar ise Bharat ke kaarigaron ka kamaal maante hain.
