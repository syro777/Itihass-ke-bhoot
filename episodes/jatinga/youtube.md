## Description
Assam ka ek gaon, jahan har saal panchhi jaise khud maut ki taraf udte hain! 😱
Jatinga (Dima Hasao) mein August se October ki andheri, kohre bhari raaton mein jhund ke jhund panchhi gaon ki roshni ki taraf udte hain — aur zameen par gir padte hain.

Aapko kya lagta hai — BHOOTON KA SAAYA ya KUDRAT KI ULJHAN? 👇 Comment mein batao!

📜 Facts: Jatinga, Assam ke Dima Hasao zile mein Haflong ke paas hai. Ye ghatna aam taur par August se October ke beech, bina chaand wali, kohre aur halki baarish wali raaton mein hoti hai. Gaon waale pehle ise buri aatmaon ka kaam maante the. Vaigyanikon ke mutabik kohra aur tez hawa panchhiyon ko bhatka deti hai, aur roshni unhe kheench leti hai; pakshi visheshagya Anwaruddin Choudhury ke anusaar zyadatar ye chhote, sthaniya panchhi hote hain. Pehle thake panchhiyon ko baans ke dandon se maara jaata tha; ab van vibhaag pehra deta hai aur jagrukta badhi hai.
🐦 Ye panchhi "suicide" nahi karte — ye ek prakritik bhatkav hai.
