Pakistan border ke paas bane is mandir ke aangan mein kareeb 450 gole gire, par mandir ko kharonch tak nahi aayi. Aur wo bina phate gole aaj bhi mandir ke museum mein rakhe hain.

📜 Facts: Tanot Mata (Tanot Rai) Mandir Rajasthan ke Jaisalmer zile mein, India–Pakistan border ke paas hai. 1965 ki jung mein yahan bhaari golabaari hui. "3000 bam" aur "450 gole" ke aankde local kahaniyon aur reports mein bataye jaate hain; inka koi official military count saamne nahi aaya. Grih Mantralaya (PIB, 2022) ki release ke mutabik mandir parisar par gire gole phate nahi. Bina phate gole mandir ke museum mein rakhe hain. 1971 ki Longewala jeet ke baad yahan Vijay Stambh banaya gaya, aur aaj mandir ki dekhrekh aur pooja BSF ke jawan karte hain.
