📜 Facts: 1665 ki Purandar sandhi ke baad Shivaji Maharaj 1666 mein Agra mein Aurangzeb ke darbar pahunche. Wahan unhe chhote sardaaron ki kataar mein khada kiya gaya; naraaz hokar wo darbar chhodkar nikle aur unhe nazarband kar diya gaya. Unhone bimaari ka bahaana kiya aur daan ke liye mithai ki badi tokriyan bahar bhejni shuru ki. Pehle pehredaar tokriyan jaanchte the, phir jaanchna band ho gaya. August 1666 mein Shivaji aur Sambhaji tokriyon mein chhupkar nikal gaye, jabki sautele bhai Hiroji Farzand unke bistar par chaadar odhkar lete rahe. Shivaji sadhu ke bhes mein Maharashtra laute, aur 1674 mein Raigad par unka rajyabhishek hua.
