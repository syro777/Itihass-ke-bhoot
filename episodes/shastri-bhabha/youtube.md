## Description
January 1966… sirf 13 din mein Bharat ne apne do sabse bade log kho diye! 😱
Pehle Tashkent samjhaute ke kuch ghante baad PM Lal Bahadur Shastri ki achanak maut. Phir theek 13 din baad, Bharat ke parmanu karyakram ke janak Dr. Homi J. Bhabha ka Air India vimaan Alps ke Mont Blanc se takra gaya.

📜 Facts: Shastri ji ki maut 11 January 1966 ko Tashkent mein hui; sarkaari wajah dil ka daura bataayi gayi. Na Bharat mein postmortem hua, na Soviet Union mein. Unki patni Lalita Shastri ne zehar ka shak jataaya tha. 1977 ki Raj Narain committee ke records, CIC (2018) ke mutabik, milte nahi hain.
Dr. Homi Bhabha ki maut 24 January 1966 ko Air India Flight 101 ke crash mein hui (117 log). France ki jaanch ke mutabik wajah pilot ki navigation galti thi. Bhabha ne All India Radio par kaha tha ki manzoori mile to Bharat 18 mahine mein atom bam bana sakta hai.
