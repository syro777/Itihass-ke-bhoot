📜 Facts: Sree Padmanabhaswamy Mandir Thiruvananthapuram (Kerala) mein Bhagwan Vishnu ka mandir hai. 2011 mein Supreme Court ke aadesh par iske tehkhanon (vaults) ki inventory hui, jo poorv IPS adhikari T.P. Sundararajan ki yachika se shuru hui thi. Paanch tehkhanon se sone ki murtiyan, sikke, keemti patthar aur gehne mile; media anumaan ke mutabik inki keemat 1 lakh crore rupaye (lagbhag 22 billion dollar) se zyada hai, koi official valuation jaari nahi hua. Tehkhana B nahi khola gaya; Travancore shahi parivaar ne iska virodh kiya, aur iske darwaze par naag jaisi aakriti ki vajah se kai lokkathayein hain (kehte hain zabardasti kholne par anarth hoga). Sundararajan ji ka July 2011 mein bukhaar ke baad nidhan hua, jise afwaahon ne 'shraap' se joda; iska koi saboot nahi. July 2020 mein Supreme Court ne Travancore shahi parivaar ke adhikaar barkaraar rakhe aur Tehkhana B ka faisla mandir samiti par chhoda. 2025 mein bhi ise kholne ki maang uthi; tehkhana aaj bhi band hai.
