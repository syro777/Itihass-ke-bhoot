📜 Facts: February 1959 mein Russia ke Northern Ural pahaadon mein Igor Dyatlov ki team ke 9 hikers ki maut ho gayi. Tent andar se kata mila, saamaan wahin chhoda gaya tha, aur kai log sirf mozon mein ya nange pair bhaage the. Zyadatar ki maut thand (hypothermia) se hui, kuch ko gehri chotein thi. Theories aliens se lekar hathiyaar test aur yeti tak gayi. 2019 ki Russian jaanch ne vajah avalanche bataayi, aur 2021 mein Johan Gaume (EPFL) aur Alexander Puzrin (ETH Zurich) ke model ne dikhaaya ki ek chhota 'slab avalanche' ye sab samjha sakta hai. Phir bhi khud vaigyanik kehte hain ki poora sach shayad kabhi pata na chale.
