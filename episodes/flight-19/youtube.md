📜 Facts: 5 December 1945 ko US Navy ke 5 TBM Avenger torpedo bombers (Flight 19) Fort Lauderdale, Florida se ek routine training flight par nikle. Leader Lt. Charles Taylor ne radio par bataya ki unke compass kaam nahi kar rahe. Squadron samundar ke upar raasta bhatak gaya aur fuel khatam hone ki aashanka hai. Unhe dhoondhne gaya PBM Mariner plane bhi 13 logon ke saath kho gaya (maana jaata hai ki usmein aag lagi). Kul 27 log gaye, aur Flight 19 ka malba aaj tak nahi mila. Navy jaanch ne vajah bataayi: raasta bhatakna aur kharaab mausam. Bermuda Triangle ki dehshat isi ghatna se mashhoor hui, par NOAA ke mutabik is ilaake mein haadse baaki samundar se zyada nahi hote.
